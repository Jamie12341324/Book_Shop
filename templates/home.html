Book Shop Home.

Book shop is a place to buy and sell non-fiction and fiction books. This is partly to help people feel more enlightened and intelligent, while feeling like they are progressing through life. One purpose is to help people buy books online so that they don't have to drive to their local book shop to get books if they even have one.

Status of reading

For many book sellers most of the books they sell are fiction. Book Shop aims to help with this by selling mostly non-fiction books to balance out the reading habits of the public.

Why read non-fiction?

To educate yourself and the world about science so that science can advance and help improve peoples lives by helping to do new things and old things more effectively.

The effects of reading

  • Strengthens the brain
  • Increases empathy
  • Builds vocabulary
  • Improves sleep
  • Lengthens lifespan
